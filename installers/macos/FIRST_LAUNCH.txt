Aura — first launch on macOS
============================

1. Drag Aura onto the Applications folder in this window.

2. Aura is not yet signed with an Apple Developer ID, so macOS quarantines it
   after download and may refuse to open it ("Aura is damaged and can't be
   opened", or "Apple could not verify Aura is free of malware").

   To clear the quarantine flag, open Terminal and run:

       xattr -cr /Applications/Aura.app

3. Open Aura normally from Applications or Launchpad.

You only need to do step 2 once, after installing or updating.


Wrong architecture?
-------------------
Aura ships a separate build for each Mac chip:

    Aura-macOS-AppleSilicon.dmg   Apple M1, M2, M3, M4 and newer
    Aura-macOS-Intel.dmg          Intel Macs (roughly 2020 and earlier)

To check which you have: Apple menu > About This Mac. If the app quits right
away or reports a bad CPU type, you most likely have the other build.

    https://github.com/Etriti00/aura-app/releases/latest
